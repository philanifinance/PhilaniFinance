02 · The problem
01 · Six bottlenecks holding the business back

What's working against you today.

Without a purpose-built digital platform, each loan application touches multiple manual steps, compliance risks, and communication gaps that compound with volume. Six structural issues cost time, create risk, and limit growth.

01
Manual, paper-based loan applications
Applications are collected on paper or via email, requiring manual data entry, physical document handling, and repeated back-and-forth with applicants. This slows processing, introduces errors, and limits throughput.
02
No client self-service portal
Applicants have no visibility into their application status after submission. This generates high volumes of follow-up phone calls, WhatsApp messages, and in-person visits — consuming staff time that could be spent on credit assessment.
03
Ad-hoc compliance & audit trail
The NCA requires a clear, auditable record of every lending decision including pre-agreement statements, credit checks, and signed contracts. Without a centralised system, this trail is fragmented across emails, files, and handwritten notes.
04
Insecure document collection
Collecting payslips, bank statements, and ID documents via WhatsApp or email is a POPIA compliance risk. Sensitive personal data flows through uncontrolled channels with no encryption, access logging, or retention policy.
05
No automated client notifications
Status updates — approval, document requests, contract readiness, DebiCheck alerts — are communicated manually by phone or WhatsApp. Delays in communication reduce client trust and increase churn at critical decision points.
06
Zero portfolio analytics
Without a central dashboard, management has no real-time view of pipeline volume, approval rates, active loan values, repayment trends, or month-on-month application growth. Strategic decisions are made without data.
