Design notes

How It Works — four steps to funds

Numbered step timeline with icons, estimated durations and color-coded cards walks applicants through the full journey: Apply (2 min) → Verify documents → Approve → Payout (same day). A feature image with floating trust badges adds credibility without clutter.

The layout adapts cleanly to mobile — steps stack vertically and the image collapses gracefully.
