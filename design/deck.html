Design notes

Landing Page — the trust moment

Dark navy hero with an embedded loan calculator — amount slider, term selector, and live repayment preview — anchors the above-the-fold experience. Animated stat counters (total loans, avg approval time, client satisfaction) build credibility on scroll.

TrustBar presents key platform metrics with brand-green glows. HowItWorks uses a numbered four-step timeline. The CtaBanner is a cinematic full-bleed photo section with an NCR compliance badge and a single strong CTA. Every element is scroll-triggered with reveal animations.

How It Works — four steps to funds

Numbered step timeline with icons, time estimates and color-coded cards walks applicants through the journey: Apply (2 min) → Verify → Approve → Payout (same day). A feature image with floating trust badges adds credibility without clutter.

The layout adapts cleanly to mobile — steps stack vertically and the image collapses gracefully below the timeline.
